## Brand & Style

This design system embraces an ultra-refined, conversational minimalism paired with fluid, ambient illumination. Drawing direct inspiration from modern AI conversation spaces and high-utility consumer creative tools, the aesthetic strips away typical SaaS clutter, dense dashboards, and mechanical widgets in favor of expansive dark canvases, crisp geometric typography, and intentional moments of vivid chromatic energy.

### Design Movement & Mood
- **Minimalist Dark Canvas:** Deep, velvety nocturnal tones (#0B0D12 and #141720) create an atmosphere of quiet focus, calm confidence, and effortless utility.
- **Fluid Luminous Accents:** Multi-stop chromatic gradients transitioning across Electric Indigo, Violet, Vibrant Pink, and Warm Amber serve as an energetic spark—evoking creation, infinite possibility, and magic without compromising reading comfort.
- **Friendly Precision:** Generous border radii (16px–20px), airy whitespace, and soft, tactile micro-surfaces balance engineering rigor with friendly consumer approachability.

## Colors

The color palette is architected for maximum legibility in an immersive dark canvas, using high-impact chromatic points sparingly to guide intent and celebrate user milestones.

### Core Tonal Palette
- **Base Canvas (`#0B0D12`):** Ground level for the overall viewport and base shell.
- **Surface Elevation (`#141720`):** Floating card backgrounds, sheets, and modular container surfaces.
- **Elevated Interactive (`#1E2230`):** Hover states, input backdrops, and active toggle planes.
- **Hairline Border (`#252936`):** Ultra-subtle, low-contrast structural outlines separating tonal tiers.

### Text & Contrast
- **Text Primary (`#F7F7F8`):** Uncompromised crispness for headlines, titles, and active inputs.
- **Text Secondary (`#9CA3AF`):** Soft, muted gray for meta-labels, placeholders, and descriptions.
- **Text Tertiary (`#64748B`):** Ghost text, disabled states, and auxiliary timestamps.

### Accent & Gradient Spectrum
- **Electric Indigo (`#6366F1`):** Logic, primary action trigger, focus rings.
- **Innovation Violet (`#A855F7`):** Secondary accent, active creative states.
- **Vibrant Pink (`#FF6B8B`):** Expressive highlight, notifications, gradient midpoint.
- **Warm Amber (`#FBBF24`):** Warmth, badges, spark indicators, gradient terminus.
- **Signature Aurora Gradient:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #6366F1 0%, #A855F7 35%, #FF6B8B 70%, #FBBF24 100%)`, reserved strictly for brand hero moments, key primary CTAs, active generation states, and luminous ambient underglows.

## Typography

The typography strategy leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** uniformly across all functional layers, bringing friendly geometric precision and superior on-screen clarity.

### Hierarchy & Treatment
- **Display & Headlines:** Set with tight tracking (-0.03em to -0.01em) and strong weights (700–800) to create impact without visual aggression. Hero titles often pair solid `#F7F7F8` text with inline gradient-clipped text for rhythmic punctuation.
- **Body:** Kept intentionally airy with generous line height (1.6x) to facilitate comfortable scanning of conversational replies, contextual descriptions, and instructions.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Crisp medium-to-bold weights with subtle tracking expansion ensure immediate optical legibility across compact mobile touch targets and status tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces mobile-first conversational utility, borrowing the wide, calming gutters and centered column structures of modern generative intelligence interfaces.

### Breakpoints & Adaptive Rhythm
- **Mobile (<640px):** Single-column vertical stream. Margin is set to `1.25rem` (20px), gutter is `1rem` (16px). All interactive inputs sit anchored to the bottom thumb zone with integrated home-bar clearance.
- **Tablet (640px–1024px):** Single centered reading column (maximum width 680px) to prevent over-extended line lengths, or an asymmetric 2-column layout (navigation rail + workspace).
- **Desktop (>1024px):** Centered application frame maxing out at 1120px for dashboards or 768px for conversational and creation flows, with `margin-desktop: 2.5rem` and clean perimeter padding.

### Spacing Principles
- Never crowd content. Generous vertical spacing (`space-xl`) between conversational segments or cards creates a deliberate, distraction-free environment.
- Micro-grouping uses `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for tightly coupled iconography and labeling.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows and faux-3D layering in favor of **ambient luminosity** and **subtle tonal planes**.

### Depth Layers
- **Level 0 (Floor):** Pure `#0B0D12` matte ground.
- **Level 1 (Card & Bubble Surface):** `#141720` backed by a 1px continuous stroke of `#252936`.
- **Level 2 (Active Overlays & Floating Prompts):** `#1A1E2B` with a dual border: an inner 1px translucent white stroke (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`) and an ultra-diffused atmospheric shadow (`0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`).
- **Level 3 (Signature Aurora Glow):** Applied to floating action triggers and hero assets. A low-opacity, wide-radius colored blur (`blur(40px) to blur(80px)`) tinted with `#6366F1` and `#FF6B8B` positioned behind interactive hubs, giving the illusion that the UI produces internal light.

## Shapes

The shape system is friendly, soft, and consistent:
- **Default Elements (`rounded-lg` / 16px):** Primary content cards, prompt inputs, modal dialogs, and image containers.
- **Expansive Containers (`rounded-xl` / 24px):** Screen-bottom action sheets, hero media panels, and major feature cards.
- **Pill & Floating Controls (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Action buttons, chip filters, status tags, and avatar rings.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Gradient CTA:** Full pill (`rounded-full`), filled with the four-stop Aurora gradient (`#6366F1` to `#FBBF24`), text `#FFFFFF` (semibold), with an ambient gradient glow on hover/active.
- **Secondary / Ghost Surface:** Floating pill in `#141720` with a 1px border of `#252936`. Text is `#F7F7F8`. On press, surface transitions smoothly to `#1E2230`.
- **Icon Utility Buttons:** 44x44px minimum circular touch targets with subtle `#252936` perimeter and muted icon color (`#9CA3AF`), brightening to `#F7F7F8` on interaction.

### Input Fields & Prompt Bars
- **Floating Command Bar:** Height 56px, `rounded-2xl` (20px), surface `#141720`, border 1px `#252936`. Inside padding `16px 20px`. Placeholder text `#9CA3AF`. Right-aligned action button integrated seamlessly into the pill shell.
- **Active Focus:** Focus border shifts from `#252936` to `#6366F1` with an outer soft glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.2)`.

### Cards & Containers
- **Utility Cards:** `#141720` surface, 16px–20px corner radius, 1px `#252936` stroke, no default drop shadow.
- **Hero / Milestone Cards:** Features a dark gradient overlay underneath content with a luminous bottom or top border highlight derived from the signature accent gradient.

### Chips & Filter Tabs
- **Interactive Chips:** 36px height, `rounded-full`, `#141720` base, 1px border. Selected state utilizes an active gradient fill or solid `#6366F1` fill with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Checkboxes, Radios, & Switches
- **Switches:** 50x30px pill container, inactive background `#1E2230`, active background filled with `#6366F1` to `#A855F7`. Thumb is pure white `#FFFFFF`, 24px circular, sliding smoothly with cubic-bezier easing.
- **Checkboxes:** 22x22px square with 6px rounded corners. Inactive `#141720` with `#252936` border. Active filled with `#6366F1` featuring a crisp white check icon.

### Navigation & Bottom Dock
- **Floating Island Dock:** Floating 16px above the home bar, blur-backed surface `rgba(20, 23, 32, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, rounded-full, housing essential destination icons with clean dot indicators for active states.
